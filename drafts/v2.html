Hi, I’m Mikalai.

Where I’m from

I grew up in Belarus.

in a city where everyone knows someone who knows you.

What I used to do

sport, seriously, for years.

then 5 years as a product manager.

as usually I was good in preparing the requirements and adjusting the vision, haha.

and I still don’t know SQL.

What I do now

I build small startups and test them in public.

the first one is Nodly ↗ - a Slack bot that helps coworkers meet around things they love outside work.

in 2026 I pushed my first MR in my life, and now I write most of its code myself, with AI.

this month I’m testing an AI that listens.

Where I’m at now

I live in Warsaw and I’m learning to sell.

zero paying customers so far.

yes, it’s a little embarrassing to write it here.

but that’s how it is.

Why I build in public

I was afraid to talk to customers.

so I kept building instead.

I envied founders who try idea after idea until one of them works.

so now I test my ideas where you can watch.

if I can fail fast in public, you can try your idea too.

good luck!
