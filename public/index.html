Why I built this

Hi, I'm Mikalai , and I build Nodly in public.

for a long time I was afraid to show anything. but the best defense is offense, so I built this site.

I built it for three things:

  1. Show the fails. tell everyone about my mistakes and fuckups, and track it all in one place.
  2. Fight the fear. beat my own fear, and maybe inspire others to fight theirs and build something too.
  3. Find my people. people who also want to make something useful and cool, and have fun doing it.

Oh, and with Claude and ChatGPT, it's the best time for non-technical people to build something.

So I made this fuckup counter. it goes up every time I mess up.

06 Fuckup counter fuckups so far · latest: value
Lessonfind high value for the client at a low cost for you. it's a craft you only learn by doing.
Lessonset a price from day one. a trial is fine, but objections tell you the right price.
Lessonthe one who does wins. act in uncertainty and fear, talk later.
Lessonif it can be sold, even crooked, sell it now.
